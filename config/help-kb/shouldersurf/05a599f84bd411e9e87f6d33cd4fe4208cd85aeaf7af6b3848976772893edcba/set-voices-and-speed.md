**Picking a voice.** Settings > Audio > Text-to-Speech > Voice. The list is the voices installed on your iPhone, grouped Premium, Enhanced and Standard, each with a preview button. Premium voices sound best and are worth the download: iOS Settings > Accessibility > Read & Speak > Voices (on older iOS versions, Spoken Content > Voices). New voices show up in the app's list on their own.

**English only, for now.** Read-aloud uses English voices even when the meeting or your phone is in Spanish, French or Japanese.

**Speed.** Not in Settings. It's the tortoise-to-hare control on every answer's transport bar: 1x, 1.2x, 1.5x, 2x. Changing it mid-sentence restarts that sentence at the new speed. Until you touch it, 1x follows the rate you've set in iOS Accessibility > Spoken Content, so if you already read fast there, the app does too.

**When it plays.** Only when you press Play, and only through Bluetooth headphones unless Speaker Playback is on. See "Hearing answers through AirPods."

The voice and speed sync to your other devices through iCloud when iCloud Sync is on.
