You're recording through the Mac or Windows Companion and a banner appears: "Lost the connection to your computer. Recording with the iPhone mic while we reconnect." Here's what's going on and what to do.

**Nothing is lost.** The instant the link drops, the phone's own mic takes over, so the recording and transcript continue. The banner has a Dismiss button; dismissing hides it and the app keeps trying.

**It reconnects on its own.** The app retries after a few seconds, then every 10 seconds. When the computer is back, the mode flips to the Companion again and a short "Reconnected to your computer" note shows. A brief Wi-Fi blip usually fixes itself in under ten seconds. If the computer drops three times in five minutes, the app rests on the phone mic for five minutes before trying again, and the banner says so.

**At the start of a meeting** the rule is different: the phone waits up to eight seconds for the computer. If it doesn't answer, recording starts on the phone mic and a sheet asks whether to keep going that way ("Keep recording with iPhone Mic") or end.

**Other banners you may see,** each with a "Use iPhone Mic" button: "Not paired with your computer," "Allow screen and audio recording on your Mac," "Computer not connected," "Your computer is not sending audio." They all mean the same thing: the phone is recording with its own mic, and the Companion needs attention on the computer side.

**Things to check on the computer.** The Companion icon is in the menu bar or tray and says Connected or Relaying. Screen & System Audio Recording and Microphone are allowed. The Mac and the phone are on the same Wi-Fi (not one on a guest network). On Windows, the firewall allowed Private networks.

**Pairing again.** Settings > Audio > Computer > Shoulder Surf Companion shows the paired computer and the ones on the network. "Reset the shared key" and a fresh six-digit code from the computer's menu re-pair from scratch.
