Settings > Camera.

**When a meeting starts.** Whether the camera is on as recording begins: On (the default), Off, or Same as last meeting, which remembers what you did with the camera button last time. Off saves battery and keeps the phone cooler. You can still switch the camera on or off any time during a meeting, and Read the screen always uses it. This choice stays on each device; it doesn't sync.

**Capture Mode.** Audio (the normal mode) or Captions, which is Read the screen: the camera reads live captions off a screen instead of recording audio. "Set captions area" lets you mark where the captions sit for your meeting platform, once per layout. "Show Captions Setup Guide Again" replays the walkthrough.

**Image Quality.** How big a photo is when it's sent with a question. On your own key: Original, 2048, 1024 or 512 px on the longest side. Smaller is cheaper and still plenty for slides and charts; 1024 is the default. On Shoulder Surf AI it's managed by your plan and the row just shows the size. This only affects what's sent to the AI; the photos saved with the meeting are full size.

**Reset Confirmation Prompts** brings back the one-time cards the app shows (like the "Photos give the AI something to look at" card when you first turn the camera off).

Turning the camera off partway through a meeting is done on the recording screen; see "Capturing slides and screens."
