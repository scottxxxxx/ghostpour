The Transcript section on a meeting page is the full text with speakers, timing and the audio.

**Reading it.** Long turns are broken into paragraphs at natural pauses, and the speaker label repeats every so often so you never lose track of who's talking. If the server returned a cleaned-up version of the transcript (punctuation, fewer stumbles), that's what you see. "Find in transcript" searches with previous and next arrows.

**Timestamps and playback.** Each block shows its time (m:ss) when the recording exists. Tap a block and the audio jumps there. The player has play/pause, 15-second skips, speed (1x to 2x), and "Follow Playhead" to keep the text scrolling with the audio.

**Skip silence.** The player jumps over dead air by default, pauses longer than 5 seconds. The menu lets you choose 3, 5, 10 or 30 seconds, or Off. It only affects playback; the recording isn't edited.

**Speakers.** Tap any speaker label for a menu: "This is <name>" for anyone the app already knows, Name Speaker / Rename Speaker (everywhere in the meeting, or only this section), "Not <name>" to unassign a passage, and Enroll Voice to teach the app that voice for future meetings. Renaming also updates the meeting's memories. If a first name matches more than one person in your memory, the app asks "Which <name>?" so it files things under the right person. "Restore Original Speaker Labels" in the ⋯ menu goes back to Speaker 1, Speaker 2.

**Copying.** ⋯ menu > Copy Transcript copies the raw transcript text (not the cleaned-up version shown on screen). The Export Artifacts sheet writes it as a .txt file named after the meeting, or as subtitles (.srt).

**Fixing a misheard word** everywhere in the meeting at once: see "Fix a Word."

**Language.** The globe chip beside the tags switches the transcript (and the rest of the meeting) between the original and a translation. See "Reading a meeting in another language."
