A report is the structured write-up of a meeting: a title and category, a summary, who was there, a red / yellow / green status, a sentiment score with its arc across the meeting, Action Required (owner, task, priority, deadline), decisions, open questions, technical issues, and the questions you asked during the meeting. It's written on Shoulder Surf's servers from the transcript the app sent at the end, so it needs an account.

**When it's automatic.** At the end of a meeting of five minutes or more with a real amount of talking, when you're signed in with Apple. The post-meeting screen shows "Generating meeting report..." and after ten seconds lets you choose Keep Waiting or "Tag Now, View Later." When it lands you get the report review: edit the Action Required, Decisions, Open Questions and Tags sections, then share.

**When it's manual.** The "Generate your meeting report" banner on the meeting page, the "Generate full report" button on the card, or Regenerate Report in the ⋯ menu. Any meeting over about half a minute qualifies.

**On Apple Cloud or your own key,** reports are still written by Shoulder Surf AI and use your account's monthly allowance. Once the Free allowance is spent, reports stop being automatic for those models, but the manual button still works and tells you if the allowance is the problem.

**How many on Free.** The Free plan includes a set number of reports a month, and Apple's models get fewer than Shoulder Surf AI (the plan cards show today's numbers). If you hit the limit on Apple's model, the report says so and offers to switch you to Shoulder Surf AI and write it there.

**Sharing.** The Meeting Report card has Edit and Share. Share hands the iOS share sheet an HTML page, an image, and a PDF, plus plain, Markdown and Slack text versions. Every report says "Powered by Shoulder Surf" at the bottom.

**Language.** The report is written in your phone's language. If the meeting was spoken in a different language, the meeting page asks which one to write the summary and report in (see "Reading a meeting in another language"). Switching a meeting to another language regenerates the report in that language.

**Free plan, over the allowance.** You get a sample report with a SAMPLE badge and a "Generate Full Report" or "Upgrade" button. It regenerates itself once you have allowance again.

**If it fails,** see "The report didn't generate."

**Signed out?** The gate page explains: "Reports are written from your meeting on our servers. Sign in with Apple to turn them on," with a "See a sample report" link. Meetings recorded while signed out never reached the server, so they show "Report unavailable" permanently.
