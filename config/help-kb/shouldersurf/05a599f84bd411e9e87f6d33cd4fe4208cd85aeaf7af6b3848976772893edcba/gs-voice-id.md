Voice ID is how the app knows which speaker is you. Enroll once and your lines in every transcript are labelled "You" instead of "Speaker 2," and memory knows which promises are yours.

**Enroll.** Settings > Voice ID > Enroll Voice. You read a few phrases aloud for about 30 seconds. A level meter nudges you ("Speak a bit louder," "Too loud!"). Tap Save Voice. Setup also offers this on the "Let's learn your voice" page; skipping there is fine, you can do it any time.

**A reminder in Memory.** If Memory is on for your account and you haven't enrolled, a "Memory works best when it knows your voice" card shows at the bottom of Memory. More info explains why and has Enroll My Voice. Close it and it waits a few days; close it a few times and it stops.

**Mark yourself.** The profile with the "You" badge is the owner. If you enrolled someone else first, or have several profiles, tap "This Is My Voice" on the right one.

**Other people.** You can enroll colleagues too, from a meeting: open the transcript, tap a speaker's name, and choose "Enroll Voice." Their future meetings get labelled automatically. Profiles can be renamed (pencil) or deleted (swipe). "Delete All Profiles" wipes them; you can always re-enroll.

**Test Voice ID** on the same screen runs a quick check. If two profiles sound alike, a "Recognition Warnings" section tells you.

**Improve it.** Re-enrolling replaces your saved voice; recording again in a quieter spot is the way to get better matches.

**Where it lives.** Enrollment audio and the voice fingerprints stay on the phone. If iCloud Sync is on, they ride along to your other devices through your private iCloud, so you don't re-enroll on the iPad.

If you skip Voice ID entirely, the app still separates speakers; it just calls them Speaker 1, Speaker 2, and so on until you name them.
