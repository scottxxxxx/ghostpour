The timer is running but the transcript stays empty. Work down the list.

**1. Is the mic muted?** The mic circle at the top right should be green. Red with a slash means muted; tap it. In Read the screen mode it's slashed on purpose, because that mode records no audio at all; switch the listening pill to iPhone Mic if you wanted sound.

**2. Did iOS get permission?** Settings > Privacy & About > Permissions shows Microphone and Speech Recognition. Either one "Denied" means no transcript. Fix in iOS Settings > Shoulder Surf.

**3. Can the phone hear it?** The "Move closer or turn up volume" pill at the start of a session means the signal is weak. Put the phone nearer the people or the laptop speakers, and turn the laptop volume up. If AirPods are in, remember they're not the mic; the phone is.

**4. Is Voice Isolation on?** If the idle screen showed "Voice Isolation is on," the phone is filtering out every voice but yours. Tap Change and pick Standard or Wide Spectrum.

**5. Is the language right?** The speech engine listens for one language. If the meeting is in Spanish and the globe badge under the orb says English, you'll get gibberish or nothing. Pick the spoken language before starting. The first use of a new language downloads its speech model, which needs a connection; recording starts anyway and the transcript catches up once the model lands.

**6. Is it a cable or companion mode?** In USB-C mode a pulled cable shows "Cable unplugged" and falls back to the phone mic; plug it back in. In Companion mode, "Your computer is not sending audio" means the Mac or PC hasn't been given Screen & System Audio Recording permission, or the call is playing through a device the computer isn't capturing.

**7. Did something interrupt?** A phone call or another app taking the mic shows "Recording paused" and resumes on its own. If it didn't, pause and resume from the orb.

**8. Was the app in the background a long time?** Audio keeps recording, but when you return the speech engine restarts; a few words at the seam can be lost. That's normal.

If none of that helps, write to support@shouldersurf.com and tell us what you saw.
