Three plans on Shoulder Surf AI. Prices show in the app from the App Store (Plus and Pro are monthly subscriptions; Plus has a one-week free trial).

**Free.** A live transcript and summary with who said what. Shareable meeting reports, a set number each month (more on Shoulder Surf AI than on Apple's models), shareable as a link in any language. People pages. iCloud sync and backup. A monthly allowance of cloud AI, about five hours' worth, to try the rest: live questions, Meeting Chat, Project Chat over the last 10 days of meetings, web searches and file generations within the monthly allowance, and a photo with your question. Memory shows as a preview. The allowance resets monthly; when it runs out, switch to on-device or your own key until it does, or upgrade.

**Plus.** After any free trial, no monthly cap on questions, during the meeting and after. Memory (Context Quilt) on: commitments, preferences and blockers tracked across meetings and brought into every answer. Project Chat across the last 30 days of meetings. Attach documents and the answer reads every page. More web searches each month. Turn any chat into a real Excel, Word, PowerPoint or PDF file. Several images per question.

**Pro.** Everything in Plus, with a stronger model writing your reports and post-meeting analysis. Project Chat across every meeting you've ever recorded, with room for long meetings and big documents in one question. Your choice of summary style (full, or just what's new). The most web searches, and more images per question.

**Not tied to a plan.** Recording, transcription, speaker separation and Voice ID are on the phone and free. Reports, sharing and People are on every plan. Apple On-Device and Apple Cloud are free models with their own limits; your own key is billed by that provider. The one-time Extras packs for Apple Cloud and own keys are separate from these plans; see "Extras packs."

**Where to see it.** Settings > Account and Settings > AI Model both show your plan and the plans above it. On Free, both also show a usage meter that turns orange past 80%.

The exact allowances (web searches, file generations, images per question, the Free allowance) are set on the server, shown live in the app, and may be adjusted.
