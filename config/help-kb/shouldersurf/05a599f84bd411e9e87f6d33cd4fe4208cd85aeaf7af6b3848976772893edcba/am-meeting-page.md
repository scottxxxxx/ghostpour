Open a meeting from Review and you get everything the app kept, top to bottom:

- **Project pill** (or "No Project"). Tap it to move the meeting to another project, create one, or clear it. The sentiment emoji and urgency orb sit at the right.
- **Tags**, a "+ Tag" button, and a globe chip showing the language you're reading in.
- **Report banner** when there's no report yet: "Generate your meeting report." Signed out, it says "Report unavailable."
- **Confirm cards** ("From this meeting, N to confirm"): things this meeting seems to have finished. Tap "Yes, it did" or "Not yet."
- **Alignment card**, only when the meeting changed a project's direction.
- **Chat**: ask about this meeting.
- **Meeting Report**: view, edit or share the report.
- **Summary**: the final summary plus a timeline of the summaries made during the meeting.
- **Transcript**: with speakers, timestamps and the audio player.
- **Queries**: everything you asked during the meeting and the answers.
- **Images**: photos you captured.
- **Memories**: what Memory took from this meeting (Plus and Pro).
- **Links**: web addresses found in your photos or added by hand.

**The ⋯ menu** at the top right: Rename Meeting (leave it empty to go back to the automatic name), Copy Transcript, Fix a Word, Language, Share Meeting, Stop Sharing Link, Export Artifacts, What's the Difference?, Re-analyze Meeting (rebuilds the summary from the full transcript, then redoes sentiment, urgency, tags and title with your current AI model), Restore Original Speaker Labels, Regenerate Report, Add to Your Memory (shared meetings), and Delete Meeting.

A tip that shows up on meetings with unnamed speakers: open the transcript and tap a speaker name to name them or enroll their voice.
