**Sign out.** Settings > Account > Sign Out. It signs this device out of Shoulder Surf AI and switches your AI model away from it. Your meetings stay on the phone, and iCloud Sync and iCloud Drive Backup carry on; they're yours, not the account's. Sign back in any time with the same Apple ID and the server side (Memory, People, plan) is where you left it. A plan bought without signing in has no Sign Out button, since there's nothing to sign out of.

**Delete account.** Settings > Account > Delete Account, directly under Sign Out. It permanently deletes your Shoulder Surf AI account and everything it stores on the servers: your email, plan record, usage history, and everything Memory and People learned from your meetings. Shared links you created stop working.

The sheet has one switch: "Also delete my meetings and recordings." On, it also wipes the meetings, audio and voice profiles from this phone, from your iCloud sync copy, and from iCloud Drive Backup. Off, your meetings stay on the device and you can keep using the app signed out, on-device or with your own key.

Two things to know before you tap:

- Deleting the account does not cancel a paid subscription. Cancel it in iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions, or it keeps renewing.
- Apple will ask you to sign in with Apple once more so the app can ask Apple to disconnect the account. If that step fails, the deletion still goes through.

Deletion is immediate and can't be undone. Signing in again afterwards with the same Apple ID makes a brand-new empty account.

If support ever needs to find your account, Settings > Account shows an Account ID you can tap to copy. Support is support@shouldersurf.com.
