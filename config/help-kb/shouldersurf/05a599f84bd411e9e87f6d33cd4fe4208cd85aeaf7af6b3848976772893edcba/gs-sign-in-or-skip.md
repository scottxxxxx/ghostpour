During setup you're offered "Try Shoulder Surf AI" with a Sign in with Apple button, and a "Skip and Use On-Device AI" button under it. Both are fine. Here's the trade.

**Skip.** The app runs on Apple Intelligence on your phone (if your device supports it). Recording, transcript, speaker labels, summaries, live questions, Meeting Chat, photos, manual tags, sharing files: all there. Nothing about your meetings is sent to Shoulder Surf's servers. What you don't get: meeting reports, Project Chat, Memory and People, and translation, because those run on the server and need an account to belong to.

**Sign in.** Sign in with Apple is the only sign-in; there's no email and password. You get a free monthly allowance of cloud AI (about five hours of typical use on the Free plan), meeting reports (a monthly number on Free), Memory and People that build up across meetings, Project Chat, and translation. Signing in switches your AI model to Shoulder Surf AI, whatever you had before, your own key included; you can switch back any time in Settings > AI Model.

What signing in sends: your meeting transcripts, summaries, questions and chats go to Shoulder Surf's servers to power those features. Your audio recordings and any API keys you've typed in stay on your device (your own iCloud, if sync is on, is yours, not ours).

**Later is fine.** Settings > Account > Sign in. The paid plans don't require sign-in either; you can subscribe first and sign in afterwards to carry the plan to your other devices. Reports, Memory and People start once you sign in with Apple.

Your account is your Apple ID. Lose access to the Apple ID and nothing can bring the server side back, so keep that one safe.
