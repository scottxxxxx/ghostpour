Some answers are easier to use as a stack of cards than a paragraph, especially when you're about to speak. That's Glance.

**What you get.** Instead of prose, a deck of four or five colored cards, one idea each: a short line to say plus a few keywords. The next card peeks in from the side. Swipe or tap to step through. Cards are colored by their role in the PREP shape (Point, Reason, Avoid, Example, Close); cards with no clear role are plain slate. The full answer is always one tap away.

**Turn it on per prompt.** Settings > Quick Prompts > tap a prompt > "Answer as cue cards." It's off for every prompt by default, including the five built-ins. Typed questions are never cue cards unless you flip the switch on the answer.

**Flip any answer.** On the answer's transport bar there's a cards / full-text switch ("Show as cue cards" / "Show the full answer"). Flipping it also sets how that prompt's follow-ups and next runs look for the rest of the session.

**Read aloud.** Pressing Play on a card deck switches that answer to the full text so the highlighted-sentence karaoke works. Pause and flip back to cards whenever you like.

**On a phone,** a cue card answer opens the sheet taller than usual so the deck and its rail fit above the follow-up bar.

**After the meeting,** the cards stay with it. In Review, the meeting's Queries show the deck above the full answer.

Glance works on every AI model and plan; there's no gate on it. If a companion is showing answers on your computer, the cards show in the Mac companion's answer window (1.0.2 or later); the Windows companion shows the full text.
