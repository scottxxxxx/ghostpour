Speech recognition is great with everyday words and shaky with names. Names and Words teaches the app how you spell the ones that matter, so "Ozmo" stops turning into "Osmo."

**Add yours.** Settings > Audio > Names and Words. Type a name or word in "Add a name or word" and tap Add. The row in Settings shows how many you've added. It works on every plan, with any AI model.

**Already included.** You don't need to type the people on your People list, your project names or your Voice ID names. Those are in automatically.

**What it changes.** As each phrase comes in, the app checks it against your list and fixes near misses, so the name is spelled your way in the transcript and the captions, and from there in summaries, answers, reports and Memory. It works on imported recordings too.

**What it leaves alone.** Real dictionary words are never changed, very short names are left as heard, and short surnames only change when they match exactly apart from accents and capitals. So a Wang on your list won't turn every Wong into Wang.

**Past meetings.** The list works on meetings from now on. To fix a word in a meeting you've already recorded, use Fix a Word on that meeting.

**Where it lives.** The correcting happens on your phone. Your list syncs to your other devices through your iCloud and isn't sent to Shoulder Surf's servers as a list.
