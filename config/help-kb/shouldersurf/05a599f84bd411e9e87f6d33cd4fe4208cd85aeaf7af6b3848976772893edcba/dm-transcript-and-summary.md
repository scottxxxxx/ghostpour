Tap **Transcript** in the bottom toolbar. The sheet has two tabs.

**Transcript.** What the app has heard so far, with speaker labels. Transcription happens on the phone (Apple's on-device speech engine), so it works with no signal and nothing leaves the device just to be transcribed. Tap a speaker label to fix it on the spot: "Not <name>" or "Mark as Overlap" when two people talked at once.

**Summary.** The rolling summary. Until the first one lands the tab says "A summary will appear here once auto-summaries generate during the session." The same summary is what the Lock Screen shows while you're recording.

**How often summaries run.** It depends on the AI model you've picked in Settings:

- Shoulder Surf AI: every 10 minutes, set by your plan.
- Apple On-Device: every 5 minutes, fixed.
- Your own key: your choice in Settings > Summaries (5, 10, 15, 20 or 30 minutes).

Auto-summaries are on by default and can be turned off in Settings > Summaries. They pause on their own when the silence watchdog has decided nobody is talking, so you don't spend AI time summarizing dead air. Summaries can be "Delta" (each one adds to the last) or "Full" (each one starts over); on Shoulder Surf AI the Free and Plus plans use Delta, Pro can choose.

On a phone the sheet opens at half height so you can still see the orb and the toolbar. On an iPad it's a wide bottom panel.

Everything in the sheet is saved with the meeting, so you don't need to copy anything out during the call. See "The meeting page" for where it all goes afterwards.
