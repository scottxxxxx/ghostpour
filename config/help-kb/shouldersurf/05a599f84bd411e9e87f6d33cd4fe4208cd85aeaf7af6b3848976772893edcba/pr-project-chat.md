Project Chat is "Ask about this project": one question, answered across every meeting in the project. "What did we decide about pricing over the last month?" "Who owns the migration?" "Draft a status update from this week's meetings."

**Controls above the composer.**

- **Meetings to include.** A slider from "All (N)" down to a few, oldest to most recent, with a line saying which dates are in.
- **Context to include.** Description, Notes, Summaries, AI Responses, Full Transcripts, Context Quilt. Chips lock after your first message; start a new conversation to change them.
- **Context capacity.** A gauge of how much is going with the question. Green is fine, orange is getting full, red means trim before sending.

**The composer.** + for Take Photo, Photo Library, References & Prompts. A Web search switch. Send. While it answers, the spinner becomes Stop. Conversations are kept: Previous conversations, Share conversation, New conversation live in the toolbar, and old ones can be deleted by swiping.

**Jump to the moment.** With the Full Transcripts chip on, answers can point at the meeting and time they came from. Tap the time and that meeting's transcript opens at that spot.

**Files.** Ask for a spreadsheet, a Word doc, a deck or a PDF and the chat offers to build one ("Want the file?"). Say yes in your own words. It takes a minute or two ("Building your file…") and the result lands in References and as a chip in the chat. If you kill the app mid-build, the chat picks it up when you come back.

**Who gets what.**

- **Plus and Pro** on Shoulder Surf AI: unlimited. Plus reads meetings from the last 30 days; Pro reads every meeting you've ever recorded.
- **Free** on Shoulder Surf AI: a preview that reads the last 10 days of meetings, within the monthly allowance. When the allowance is used up, the Plus offer.
- **Apple Cloud or your own key:** Project Chat runs on that model only with the matching Extras pack (a one-time purchase). Without it, a sheet offers the pack or "Try it free with Shoulder Surf AI." Apple Cloud fits a project's worth of meetings less comfortably than Shoulder Surf AI, and the gauge will say "Too big for Apple Cloud" sooner.
- **Apple On-Device:** no. The on-device model is too small to read across a whole project. The sheet offers the Shoulder Surf AI taste instead.
- **Signed out:** sign in first.

Memory (Context Quilt) in Project Chat answers needs Shoulder Surf AI and a plan with Memory.
