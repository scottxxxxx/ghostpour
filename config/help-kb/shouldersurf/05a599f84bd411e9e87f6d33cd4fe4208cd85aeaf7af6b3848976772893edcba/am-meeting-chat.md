The Chat card on a meeting page ("Ask about this meeting") opens a conversation about that one meeting.

**Starters.** "What were the key decisions?", "Summarize the main topics", "What action items were mentioned?", "Were there any disagreements?" Or type anything.

**Context to include.** Chips at the top: Transcript, Summary, Project, Notes (all on), AI Responses (off), and Context Quilt (off; needs Shoulder Surf AI and a plan with Memory). These lock after your first message so the conversation stays consistent; "Locked while chatting" explains, and a new conversation unlocks them.

**Attach.** The + button offers Take Photo, Photo Library, Meeting Photos, and References & Prompts. Photos need a model that can see images. The web search globe shows when your plan has searches left on Shoulder Surf AI (or on an own key with the Extras pack); answers that used it are marked "Based on web search."

**Jump to the moment.** When an answer points at a spot in the meeting, the time shows as a tinted link (like 24:20). Tap it and the transcript opens right there, over the chat; Back returns to the answer. Copying or sharing the answer turns the links into plain times. Because answers with links skip inline selection, long-press the answer and pick Select Text to copy part of it.

**From an answer.** "Draft email" turns it into a Mail draft. When an answer lists action items, an "Add N action items to Reminders" chip appears.

**Which AI.** Whatever you've selected in Settings > AI Model. On-device works (it reads the summaries rather than the whole transcript, because the model is small); Apple Cloud fits most meetings; your own key and Shoulder Surf AI take the full transcript.

For questions across many meetings, use Project Chat on the project instead.
