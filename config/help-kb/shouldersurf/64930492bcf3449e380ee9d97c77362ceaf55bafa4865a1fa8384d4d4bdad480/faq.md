**Do I need an account?** No. Skip sign-in and the app runs on the phone's own Apple Intelligence, on a device that supports it. Sign in with Apple for reports, Memory, People, Project Chat, translation and the free monthly cloud AI allowance.

**Does it work offline?** Recording, transcription and speaker separation are on the phone, so yes. On Shoulder Surf AI, a question that can't reach the server is answered on the phone by Apple Intelligence when your device has it, and missing summaries and reports catch up on their own when you're back online. See "No signal or no Wi-Fi."

**Can anyone at Shoulder Surf hear my recordings?** No. Audio stays on your phone and your own iCloud. The only way audio leaves is a share link you create with "Include the audio" on.

**Does it join the call like a bot?** No. It listens from the phone beside you. It's not on the call, not in the participant list, and not in a screen share.

**Can people tell I'm using it?** Not from the meeting software. The phone on the table is the giveaway, and you should tell people you're recording anyway.

**Can I read answers on my computer instead of my phone?** Yes, with the Mac or Windows Companion: switch on "Display responses on this Mac" (or "on this PC") and the answer shows in a see-through window over your meeting. It's kept out of screen shares; see "Answers on your computer screen."

**Can I get people's photos from my Contacts?** Yes. Tap Allow on the "Add photos from Contacts" card in People, or use ⋯ > Get from Contacts on one person's page. Photos stay on your devices and your iCloud.

**Why doesn't the answer read itself out loud?** By design. Press Play. It won't talk into your meeting uninvited.

**Why is Play greyed out?** No Bluetooth headphones connected and Speaker Playback is off. Connect AirPods, or turn on Settings > Audio > Output > Speaker Playback.

**Why won't my AirPods switch to the phone?** iOS treats a recording app like a call and won't let it steal AirPods from another device. Use the route button beside Play or Control Center.

**Can I use the AirPods mic?** No. The phone's mic hears the room; AirPods mics only hear you.

**How do I pause?** Tap the orb, then Pause recording. Tap again to resume. There's no pause in the Dynamic Island, on purpose.

**How do I end a meeting?** Tap the orb, then End session. "End without saving" appears when nothing was captured.

**Where is the pause button on the Lock Screen?** There isn't one. The Live Activity is read-only so a pocket tap can't end a recording.

**Can I record a call on my iPhone?** The app can't capture the phone's own call audio (iOS doesn't allow it). Put the call on speaker, or use the Mac or Windows Companion for computer calls.

**How long can a meeting be?** Hours. The phone gets warm with the camera on; the app turns the camera off to cool down and keeps recording.

**What happens if the phone locks?** Recording continues. The camera and speaker ID pause until you come back.

**Why did the app ask if anyone was talking?** The silence watchdog. Five minutes of no speech (adjustable in Settings > Audio) and it asks whether to stop, so a forgotten recording doesn't run all day.

**How do I name a speaker?** In the transcript, tap the speaker label. "This is <name>," or Name Speaker. Enroll Voice teaches the app their voice for next time.

**How do I get labelled as "You"?** Settings > Voice ID > Enroll Voice, about 30 seconds of reading aloud.

**It heard "Cora" instead of "Kore."** ⋯ menu on the meeting > Fix a Word. One fix changes the transcript, summary, report, answers and Memory for that meeting. For a name that keeps coming up, add it to Settings > Audio > Names and Words.

**How do I share a meeting?** ⋯ menu > Share Meeting for a link, Export Artifacts for plain files. "What's the Difference?" in the same menu explains.

**Can I revoke a link?** Yes: ⋯ menu > Stop Sharing Link. Copies already delivered can't be recalled.

**Where did my report go?** Meetings under about five minutes don't get one automatically; tap "Generate your meeting report." Signed-out meetings can't have one.

**Can I import a Zoom or Voice Memos recording?** Yes. Review > Import a Recording, or share the file to "Add to Shoulder Surf."

**What's the difference between Meeting Chat and Project Chat?** Meeting Chat asks about one meeting. Project Chat asks across every meeting in a project.

**What is Memory / Context Quilt?** The app's long-term memory of decisions, commitments and people across meetings, on Plus and Pro. People pages are free with sign-in.

**Does Memory work with my own API key?** It's captured when you're signed in on any model, but only Shoulder Surf AI uses it in answers.

**Can I use ChatGPT or Claude with my own account?** Yes: Settings > AI Model > Bring Your Own Key. Keys stay in your phone's Keychain.

**What's Apple Cloud?** Apple's free cloud model on Private Cloud Compute. Needs iOS 27 and an Apple Intelligence device. See "Apple Cloud."

**What are the Extras packs?** One-time purchases that unlock Project Chat, files, analysis, streaming and more on Apple Cloud or your own key, without a subscription.

**Does it work on iPad?** Yes, same app. The listening mode says "iPad Mic."

**Is there an Apple Watch app?** Yes, a remote: capture a photo, run a Quick Prompt, play the last answer.

**Which languages?** Transcription in English, Spanish, French, Portuguese and Japanese, where your device's speech engine has them. The app itself is in English, Spanish, French, Japanese and Brazilian Portuguese. Reports and summaries in English, Spanish, French, Japanese and Brazilian Portuguese, and so are these help articles. Read-aloud voices are English only.

**How do I delete everything?** Settings > Account > Delete Account, with "Also delete my meetings and recordings" on. Cancel any subscription separately in iOS Settings.

**How do I move my meetings to a new phone?** Restore from an iCloud Backup, or sign in with Apple with iCloud Sync on. API keys need retyping.
