**Best case: restore the new iPhone from an iCloud Backup.** Everything comes back, signed in and all: meetings, audio, voice profiles, settings, even API keys. Nothing to do.

**Fresh install, then Sign in with Apple, with iCloud Sync on.** The app always starts signed out on a new install, on purpose. Sign in with the same Apple ID and you land on the same account.

Comes back from your iCloud: meetings with audio, transcripts, summaries, reports, chats, tags and projects; voice profiles; contact links on People; References; synced settings and custom tags.

Comes back from the server, because it was never only on the phone: Memory and People, share links, your plan and usage.

You type again: API keys for your own-key providers (they live only in that phone's Keychain).

Rebuilt on demand: translations (one quick server call per meeting when you open it).

Lost, and worth nothing: dismissed tips and one-time prompts (you'll see them once more), the "meeting interrupted" recovery state, and the prep card's learned time-slot habits, which relearn.

**The one thing nothing can recover** is the Apple ID itself. The account is the Apple ID. Lose access to it and the server side is gone.

**Before it's lost:** leave iCloud Sync on (Settings > Backup & Sync; it's on by default), and consider iCloud Drive Backup if you want files you can open without the app.
