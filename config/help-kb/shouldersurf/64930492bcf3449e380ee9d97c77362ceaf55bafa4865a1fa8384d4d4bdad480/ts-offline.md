You can record a meeting with no connection at all. Here's what works, what waits, and what catches up on its own.

**Works offline.** Recording, the live transcript, speaker labels and Voice ID all run on the phone. Your recording is safe either way.

**Questions during the meeting.** On Shoulder Surf AI, a question that can't reach the server is retried once, then answered on the phone with Apple Intelligence if your device has it. Those answers say so, and they're shorter because the on-device model sees less. Meeting Chat and Project Chat tell you they can't connect. Project Chat on the On-Device model keeps working offline once you've used it online.

**Your plan.** The app remembers your plan, so being offline doesn't make a paid account look like Free.

**When the meeting ends.** On Shoulder Surf AI with no connection, the app tries Apple Intelligence for the summary instead. Apple's model sometimes declines to summarize ordinary conversation; then the summary card says "Apple Intelligence declined to summarize this." Reports, translation, People and Memory need the connection and wait for it.

**When you're back online,** the app catches up by itself. For a few meetings from the last 48 hours it fills in a missing summary, the post-meeting analysis, and a report that failed to send, and Memory gets the meeting too. If you're signed in on Shoulder Surf AI, a summary the phone wrote only because you were offline is rewritten on Shoulder Surf AI, and the card says it was improved. Older meetings, or ones it didn't get to, have Generate summary on the summary card and Regenerate Report in the ⋯ menu.

**Flaky Wi-Fi** is harder than none: a request can hang before it fails. If an answer seems stuck, wait for the error, then try again once the connection settles.
