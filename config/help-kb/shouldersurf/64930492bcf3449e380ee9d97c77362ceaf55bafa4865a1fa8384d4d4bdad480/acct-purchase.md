**Subscribe.** The plan cards in Settings > Account or Settings > AI Model (signed in or not), or any upgrade sheet the app shows you. Tap a plan and Apple's purchase sheet appears. You don't have to sign in first: the app quietly creates a device-only account to hold the plan, and Apple handles the payment. Plus starts with a one-week free trial. Buying Plus or Pro switches your AI model to Shoulder Surf AI, since that's where the plan applies; you can switch back in Settings > AI Model. Reports, Memory and People start once you sign in with Apple.

**Use it on your other devices.** A plan bought without signing in works on that device. To carry it to your iPad, Settings > Account > "Use your plan on your other devices" > Sign in with Apple. Your plan moves to your Apple account. If that Apple account already has its own plan, it keeps that one.

**Restore Purchases.** On the upgrade sheet, in Settings > Account and in Settings > AI Model. Tap it on a new device or after a reinstall; it checks your App Store purchases (subscriptions and Extras packs) and reconnects them.

**Manage or cancel.** Settings > Account > Manage Subscription opens Apple's subscription management. Cancelling there stops renewal; the plan runs to the end of the period. Deleting your Shoulder Surf account does not cancel a subscription; do that in Apple's settings.

**Redeem Code.** Settings > Account > Redeem Code opens Apple's offer code sheet.

**Usage.** On Free, Settings > Account and Settings > AI Model show how much of this month's allowance you've used. Paid plans and trials don't show a meter.

**If your plan doesn't show.** The app compares what the App Store says you bought with what the server knows. When they disagree it shows "Your X subscription isn't active on this account" with Restore Purchases and Manage. Restore usually fixes it; if not, note your Account ID (Settings > Account, tap to copy) and contact support.
