Quick Prompts are the one-tap questions at the top of the Query sheet. Tap **Query** in the bottom toolbar and you'll see a grid of colored tiles. Tap one and it sends right away, using the slice of the meeting you've chosen under "How much of the meeting should I include?" (Last 2 min is the default).

The six that ship with the app:

- **Catch Me Up**: what just happened, in a few lines.
- **Help Me Respond**: a reply you could give right now.
- **What Do I Need to Do?**: your action items so far.
- **Is That Right?**: a quick check on the last claim you heard.
- **What Are We Missing?**: gaps, risks, questions nobody asked.
- **What's the Answer?**: a direct answer to the question just asked in the room.

The wording of these is maintained by Shoulder Surf and refreshed from the server, so they can improve without an app update. They all expect some meeting context; if you tap one with nothing captured yet you'll see a "No Meeting Context" alert.

**Make your own.** Settings > Quick Prompts. Tap a prompt to edit it, drag to reorder, swipe to delete, or tap Add New Prompt. Each prompt has a name, icon, color and the instruction itself, plus switches for things like "Save to Reminders," "Answer as cue cards," taking a photo with the prompt, web search, attaching References, and how much context to include. You can edit your prompts on every AI model, including Shoulder Surf AI. (The background prompts for summaries and post-meeting analysis are managed by Shoulder Surf on Shoulder Surf AI; you can only change those on your own key.)

**Other ways to fire one:** Control Option 1 through 9 on a keyboard attached to the iPhone or iPad, the same keys on the Mac Companion, or the prompt picker on the Apple Watch app.

Quick Prompt tiles never use web search; that's only for typed questions.
