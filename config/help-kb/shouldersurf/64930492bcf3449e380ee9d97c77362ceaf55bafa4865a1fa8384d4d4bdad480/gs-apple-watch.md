The Watch app installs with the phone app. It's a small remote for a meeting that's already recording on the iPhone; it doesn't record anything itself.

Three controls:

- **Capture.** Takes a silent photo with the phone's rear camera and stages it for your next question. Handy when the phone is propped up facing a screen and you don't want to reach for it.
- **Prompt picker.** Turn the Digital Crown to pick a Quick Prompt, tap to run it. The answer shows on the phone.
- **Play.** Reads the latest answer aloud through whatever the phone is playing to. Same rule as on the phone: it needs Bluetooth headphones connected, or Speaker Playback turned on.

Every control needs an active session on the phone; otherwise the buttons are disabled. Raising your wrist refreshes the state from the phone.

If you'd rather record on the Watch's own Voice Memos app when the phone isn't handy, you can import that recording afterwards from Review > Import a Recording.
