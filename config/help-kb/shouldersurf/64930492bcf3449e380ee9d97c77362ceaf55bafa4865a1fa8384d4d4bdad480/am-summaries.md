The Summary section on a meeting page has two parts.

**Final Summary.** The last rolling summary from the meeting, consolidated. Copy and Share buttons sit beside it.

**Summary Timeline.** Each auto-summary the app wrote during the meeting, with its timestamp. Ones marked "Delta" only cover what was new since the previous one.

**Why a meeting might have no summary.** Auto-summaries run every 10 minutes on Shoulder Surf AI (every 5 on-device, your choice on an own key). A meeting that ended before the first interval (or whose summary failed) says so and offers "Generate summary," whatever its length. An imported recording never had a live session, so it offers one too. Signed-in users over their monthly allowance see "Get more time."

**Offline.** Recording and the transcript don't need a connection. If the network is gone when a meeting ends on Shoulder Surf AI, the app tries Apple Intelligence on the phone for the summary instead (on devices that have it). Apple's model sometimes declines to summarize ordinary conversation; then the card says "Apple Intelligence declined to summarize this." rather than leaving you guessing.

**Catching up.** When the connection comes back, the app goes back over the last 48 hours for a few meetings that are missing a summary, the post-meeting analysis, or a report that failed to send, and fills them in on its own. If you're signed in on Shoulder Surf AI and a summary was written on the phone only because you were offline, it's rewritten on Shoulder Surf AI; the card says "Improved with Shoulder Surf AI when you came back online" and the offline version stays in the timeline.

**Banners you may see here:** "Analysis available / Analyze Now" when the summary was written on-device and a better model is available to you; "Better AI model available / See Plans"; "Speaker names updated / Re-analyze" after you've named speakers, so the summary can use the names.

**Re-analyze Meeting** (⋯ menu) regenerates the summary from the whole transcript and then redoes the sentiment, urgency, tags and suggested title, using whichever AI model you have selected right now.

**The title.** After a meeting of a couple of minutes or more, the post-meeting analysis suggests a short title. Rename it any time from the ⋯ menu or by long-pressing in the list; clear the field to go back to the automatic one.

**Settings that matter:** Settings > Meetings > Summaries: Auto Summary on or off, the strategy (On-Device, Cloud Delta, Cloud Full; on Shoulder Surf AI it's set by your plan, Pro can choose), and update frequency (own key only).
