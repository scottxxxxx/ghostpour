The People view in Review is a page for everyone your meetings mention. It's free for any signed-in account, whatever AI model you use.

**The list.** Sections for Up Next, Open Between You, Recently With, New Faces, then "See all people" (A to Z, with search and filters; it sorts the way your iPhone's Contacts app does, by last name unless you've set Contacts to first name). Swipe or long-press a row for Merge, Same person as…, Link, merge, or rename, and Not a person.

**A person's page**, top to bottom:

- Name, and their title or a one-line description. Tap the pencil to rename, merge or link. You can type their real title ("Their title," say VP of HR); it's marked "Stated, not inferred."
- Their company, under the title. Tap it to change it, or "Add a company." A company the app worked out from an email address says it's a guess.
- **Before you meet / Insights.** Patterns the app has seen across at least three separate meetings: Decision patterns, What gets action, Follow through, What stands out, Open loops, plus a trajectory comparing earlier meetings with recent ones. Every insight says what it measured. Hold one to contest it ("Not true about <name>") and the app stops using it.
- Call, Message, Email, Edit (once you've linked a contact).
- "You owe <name>" and "<name> owes you": the open ledger between you.
- Where they show up (projects), recent meetings, and hours together.

**Prep.** The Prep button builds a card for your next conversation with them. "Share contact + brief" sends the contact plus how they decide and what moves them; receipts and the full ledger stay private to you.

**Linking a contact.** Edit > Choose from Contacts, or ⋯ > Get from Contacts to let the app find their card. If you allow Contacts access, People can also fill in photos for everyone at once; "Photos, Contacts and LinkedIn for people" explains how matching works, plus Find on LinkedIn. Photo, phone, email and company are kept on your phone (and your private iCloud if sync is on), never sent to the server.

**Two spellings, one person.** When the app suspects it, the row says so and offers "One person" or "Two." The merge sheet shows the evidence and a slider ("slide to merge"), with undo. "Keep separate" remembers that, so it won't ask again. Renaming keeps the old spelling as an alias, so recall by either name still works.

**Your own voice** isn't a person here; the owner is the "You" profile in Voice ID.
