Tap **Query**, then "Ask a custom question." A field opens ("Ask anything about the meeting...") with a send arrow. Below it you choose what goes with your question.

**How much of the meeting should I include?** Pills: None, Last 2 min (the default every time you open the sheet), Last 5 min, Full, and Since query (available after your first question). The same choice applies to Quick Prompt taps.

**References.** Attach a saved reference document (a format, a brief, an FAQ) so the AI reads it alongside your question. See the References article.

**Attach Photos.** Pick from this session's photos or your Photo Library; you can send more than one photo per question. You can send a photo with no text at all. On Apple Cloud or your own key without the matching pack, you can send a photo with your question. Photos are resized before they're sent.

**Web search globe.** Left of the text field. When it's on, the answer can use a live web search. It turns itself off after each send, so flip it on again for the next question. The globe only appears when you have searches left on Shoulder Surf AI (every plan includes a monthly allowance), or on an own key that can search with the own-key pack. Quick Prompts never search.

**While it thinks,** a "Thinking..." sheet shows with a Stop button. Stop cancels the work.

**Follow-ups.** At the bottom of any answer there's a "Follow-up..." field with a + for photos. Follow-ups stay in the same thread and keep the original prompt's style. If a follow-up fails, the error shows inside the thread.

Typed questions skip the "No Meeting Context" check, so you can ask something general even before anyone has spoken. Just know that with "None" selected the AI has nothing from the room to go on.
