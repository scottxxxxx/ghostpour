Shoulder Surf Companion is a free menu bar app for your Mac. While you record, it captures the call's audio (the other people, through the Mac's sound) and you (through the Mac's mic) as two separate tracks and streams them to the phone over your Wi-Fi. That gives the cleanest audio there is, and the two tracks help the app label who's you.

**Get it.** On the Mac App Store, or from shouldersurf.com/desktop. In the app, hold the listening pill, open the Shoulder Surf Companion card, and tap "Send link" to AirDrop or message yourself the link. It needs macOS 15 or later.

**Pair it.** A Mac signed into the same Apple ID pairs by itself through iCloud Keychain. Any other computer shows a six-digit code in its menu; type it once in Settings > Audio > Computer > Shoulder Surf Companion. That screen also lists "Computers on this network," shows live level meters (You, Meeting), and has "Reset the shared key" if pairing goes sideways.

**Permissions on the Mac.** The Companion needs Screen & System Audio Recording and Microphone. The phone will say "Allow screen and audio recording on your Mac first" until it has them. On the phone, iOS asks for Local Network access so it can find the Mac.

**Use it.** Set the listening pill to Shoulder Surf Companion and tap the orb. The phone shows "Connecting to your computer…" and waits up to eight seconds. If the Mac doesn't answer in time, recording starts on the phone mic and a sheet asks whether to keep going that way or end. The Companion shows its icon in the menu bar the whole time it's sending.

**Extras on the Mac:**

- **Display responses on this Mac.** A see-through teleprompter over your meeting with the answer the phone is showing, with back and forward through this meeting's answers. It's marked private, so screen shares and screenshots leave it out (that's how it behaved in our tests); the meeting app has the final say on a Mac, so to be certain, share a window rather than your whole screen. See "Answers on your computer screen."
- **Keyboard shortcuts.** Control Option 1 through 9 fires Quick Prompt 1 through 9 on the phone from any app while a meeting records. Editable in the Companion's menu.
- **Watch the Mac screen.** During a meeting the camera button offers your Mac's displays instead of the room, so slide captures come from the screen itself. You can crop to part of a screen and the crop is remembered.

**If the link drops mid-meeting,** see "The reconnect banner."
