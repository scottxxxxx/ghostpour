Speech recognition hears "Cora" when someone says "Kore.ai," and the wrong word then shows up in the summary, the report and every answer. Fix a Word corrects it once, everywhere in that meeting.

**Where.** ⋯ menu on a meeting > **Fix a Word**.

**How.** Two fields: "What was heard" and "What was said," plus a Match case switch. Before anything changes, "Where it appears" shows how many hits are in each part of the meeting: transcript, summaries, answers, chat, tags, the report, translations. Tap **Fix Everywhere**.

**Whole words only.** "Cora" never touches "Coral," so a person actually named Cora in the same meeting is safe as long as you turn Match case on or pick a precise spelling.

**Undo.** Each fix is listed on the sheet with its own Undo, which puts the original words back.

**What it reaches.** Everything stored on your phone for that meeting, including any translations, your other devices via iCloud sync, the iCloud Drive backup, and Spotlight. When you're signed in, it also updates Memory's notes from that meeting and replaces the transcript Shoulder Surf's servers hold, so a regenerated report keeps the right word. Under your fix the sheet says how that went ("Updating your memory…", then what changed). Undo takes the same path back.

**Other meetings.** A fix is one meeting at a time. For a name or word that keeps getting misheard, add it to Settings > Audio > Names and Words and future meetings spell it your way from the start. See "Names and Words."
