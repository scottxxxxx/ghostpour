There's a Windows version of the Companion too. It's a small tray app for Windows 10 and 11 (x64 and ARM64), on the Microsoft Store.

**What it does.** The same job as the Mac Companion: it captures the call's system audio and your mic as two tracks and streams them to the phone. It can send screenshots of your displays for the camera button, and it has a see-through answer window ("Display responses on this PC") with the full text of each answer and back and forward through this meeting's answers. Windows itself keeps that window out of screen shares, recordings and screenshots. See "Answers on your computer screen."

**Pairing.** By code only; there's no iCloud on Windows. The phone finds the PC on the network, and you type the PC's six-digit code once in Settings > Audio > Computer > Shoulder Surf Companion. The first time it runs, Windows Firewall will ask; allow it on Private networks.

**What's different from the Mac.** No keyboard shortcuts for Quick Prompts on Windows (a global Control arrow combo would steal word jumps from every app). No "Present" window. If a Mac and a PC are both on the network, pick the one you want from "Computers on this network."

Once paired, everything else works the same: set the listening pill to Shoulder Surf Companion, tap the orb, and the phone mic takes over on its own if the PC drops.
