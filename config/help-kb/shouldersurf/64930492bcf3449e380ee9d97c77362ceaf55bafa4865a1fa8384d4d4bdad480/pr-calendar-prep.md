Open the Copilot tab a few minutes before a meeting and the app tries to guess which project you're walking into, so one tap starts the recording already filed.

**With no permission at all,** it learns from your own history: if Monday at 9 is usually the CBE sync, the card says "Prepping for CBE?" with a couple of open items from the last meetings. If it guesses wrong twice in a row for a time slot, it stops guessing there until the pattern comes back.

**With your calendar,** it gets much better: it sees the actual event, who's attending, and whether it's a recurring series it has matched before. Settings > Meetings > Preparation > "Use my calendar." The app asks with its own explanation before the iOS prompt. Your calendar is read on the device only; nothing from it is uploaded. "Look ahead" sets how early the card appears (5, 15, 30 minutes, or the next meeting), and "Only meetings with attendees" skips solo blocks. Google and Outlook calendars count once they're added to the iOS Calendar app.

**The card.** "Prepping for <project>?" with the meeting time and up to two recent action items, plus a warm note. Tapping the card starts recording right away, filed under that project. "Different meeting" dismisses it; "Read last meeting" opens the previous one.

**No project yet.** When a calendar event matches no project, a card offers "Create '<event name>'" so prep, notes and memory land together from the first recording. Invites from personal email domains don't trigger this.

Everything here is deterministic and on-device; no AI is involved in the guess.
