title: The translation didn't happen
category: Troubleshooting
keywords: [translation missing, not translated, sign in to translate, translation is paused, language menu missing, original, wrong language, shared meeting, set the meeting's language]

---

You opened a meeting in another language and it's still in that language. Check these.

**Are you signed in?** Translation runs on the server. Signed out, the app says "Sign in to translate." Sign in (Settings > Account) and reopen the meeting.

**Any allowance left?** "You're out of hours this month, so translation is paused." It uses the same monthly allowance as your questions. It resumes when the month resets or you upgrade.

**Is the meeting's language known?** The globe chip and the ⋯ menu's Language section only appear when the app knows what language the meeting is in. For your own recordings that's set from the language you picked when you started. A meeting shared to you by someone else sometimes arrives without a language stamp, and then there's nothing to translate from; ask the sender to set the language and re-share, or read it as is.

**Did you decline?** On a shared meeting, "Translate this meeting?" offers "Keep as sent." The Language section still lets you translate later.

**Is it already the phone's language?** The app only translates when the recording's language differs from the phone's. A Spanish meeting on a Spanish phone reads in Spanish, with no translation to make.

**It's on my iPad but not my iPhone.** Which languages exist syncs; the translated text doesn't. Open the meeting on the other device and it rebuilds the translation with one server call.

**The summary or report is still in the old language.** Those are regenerated, not translated, which takes a moment longer than the transcript. Pull down or reopen the meeting. If a report arrived after you translated, it gets translated into your reading language on its own.

**Something looks wrong in the translation.** The original is always kept. Pick the "(matches the audio)" language to read what was actually said.
