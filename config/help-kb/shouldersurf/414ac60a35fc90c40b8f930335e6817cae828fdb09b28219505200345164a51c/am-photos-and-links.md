**Images.** Every photo you captured during the meeting sits in the Images section as a grid. Tap one for full screen with swipe between photos. From there you can save it to your Photo Library or delete it ("This removes the photo from the meeting. This can't be undone."). There's no way to add photos to a meeting after it has ended; capture happens during recording.

**Image dialogue context.** When you export photos (Export Artifacts > Images), you can include an images_context.txt file that maps each photo to the 15 seconds of conversation before it and 5 seconds after. Useful if you hand the bundle to another AI tool.

**Links.** The Links card at the bottom of the meeting page holds web addresses. "Scan photos" looks through your captures on the device for URLs and QR codes and offers what it finds ("Found N in your photos"). "Add link" lets you type one. Each link has Edit, Open and Delete. Links travel with the meeting when you share it.
