A long meeting with the camera on is real work for a phone. If iOS reports the device is getting seriously warm, Shoulder Surf turns the camera off and shows a toast for a few seconds: "Camera paused to cool down. Recording continues. It turns back on once things cool, or tap the camera button."

That's all it does. Recording, transcription and speaker identification never stop for heat. Only the camera, which is the biggest drain, takes a break.

The camera comes back on its own once the phone cools. If you tap the camera button during a heat pause, it turns on right away and won't be paused again for the rest of that meeting; you've told the app you need it.

It never fires in Read the screen mode, since the camera is the whole point there.

To keep the phone cooler in the first place: turn the camera off when you don't need slides, keep the phone off a sunny table, and skip the case if it's a thick one.
