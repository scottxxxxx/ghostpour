Didn't record with the app? Bring the audio in afterwards.

**Import a Recording.** Review > Meetings > the Import menu > Import a Recording. Audio or video: m4a, mp3, wav, mp4, QuickTime. Pick a project (or none), then the app transcribes and separates speakers on the device, exactly like a live meeting. Enrolled voices are matched; everyone else is Speaker N. The post-meeting analysis runs if the recording is long enough. There's no rolling summary for imports, so the Summary section offers "Generate summary." Voice Memos on the phone or Watch, a Zoom or Teams recording, a dictaphone file: all fine.

**From the share sheet.** In Files, Voice Memos or any app, share an audio or video file and pick "Add to Shoulder Surf." You choose the project right there; the import runs the next time you open the app. Several files at once is fine.

**Import Shared Meeting (.shouldersurf file).** Someone sent you a meeting file. Tap it, or use the Import menu. A preview card shows who it's from and what's inside, with "Add to My Library." It becomes a copy in your Review with a "Shared by" badge. If you already have it, you can Overwrite or Keep Both.

**A share link.** Tap the link; the meeting downloads and the same preview card appears, no account needed. Without the app installed, the link opens a web page with the report.

**Shared meetings and your memory.** A meeting someone else recorded isn't added to your Memory unless you say so: "Add this meeting to your memory?" appears when you ask for a report or choose "Add to Your Memory" from the ⋯ menu. Translation offers also appear on open ("Translate this meeting?").
