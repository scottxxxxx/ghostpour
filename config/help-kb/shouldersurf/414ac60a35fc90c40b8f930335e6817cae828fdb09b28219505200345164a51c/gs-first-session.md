Five minutes, one real meeting. Here's the whole loop.

**1. Put in your AirPods** (or any Bluetooth headset). You don't need them to record, but you'll want them to hear answers. See "Hearing answers through AirPods."

**2. Open the Copilot tab.** The big orb in the middle is the record button. Under it sit three small badges: the project ("No Project" until you pick one), how the app will listen, and the meeting's language. "iPhone Mic" is the default way to listen and the right choice for your first time: it hears the room, or a call playing through your laptop speakers.

**3. Tap the orb.** Recording starts right away, filed the way the badges say. Want it in a project? Tap the project badge first: pick a recent one, type a new name, or open All Projects. Meeting in another language? Tap the globe badge first. The project badge goes back to No Project after each start, and you can always move a meeting later.

**4. Let it run.** Set the phone where it can hear. The live transcript is behind the Transcript button. Tap anywhere on the camera view to snap a slide. When you want help, tap **Query** and either tap a Quick Prompt tile ("Catch Me Up" is a good first one) or type your own question. The answer slides up from the bottom; press Play to hear it.

**5. End it.** Tap the orb, then "End session." Tag the meeting while it saves (or skip that). If you're signed in and the meeting ran five minutes or more, a report starts building. You land in Review on the meeting.

**6. Look around Review.** The transcript has speaker labels you can name by tapping them. The report is shareable. Chat lets you ask about the meeting afterwards.

**Permissions you'll be asked for** along the way: microphone and speech recognition (needed to record and transcribe), camera (optional, for slides), notifications (optional, for the silence watchdog). See "Permissions, and why each one is asked."

**Two things worth doing early:** set up Voice ID (Settings > Voice ID, about 30 seconds of reading aloud) so the app labels you as "You," and sign in with Apple if you want reports, memory, and the free monthly cloud AI allowance. Neither is required to record.
