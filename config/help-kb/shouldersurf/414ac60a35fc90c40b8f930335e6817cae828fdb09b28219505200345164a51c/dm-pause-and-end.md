Tap the orb (or the timer under it) while recording. A small popover opens with the project name at the top and two buttons:

- **Pause recording** / **Resume recording**
- **End session** (it reads **End without saving** if nothing has been captured yet)

The popover says it plainly: recording is still running until you choose. Pause captures nothing until you resume, and the timer holds. End saves the meeting to Review. Tapping outside the popover keeps recording.

**While paused,** an amber bar sits across the top: "Paused, nothing is being recorded," with the time you've captured so far and Resume and End buttons. Pause also works in Read the screen mode.

**When you end:**

- If the meeting is empty (no transcript, no photos, no answers), nothing is saved and you land back on the idle screen with a short "Nothing was captured, so no meeting was saved" note.
- Otherwise the orb slides to the center, a "Saving meeting" ticker runs, and the "How'd it go?" bubble lets you tag the meeting while it saves (Add Tag, Done).
- For meetings of about five minutes or more, when you're signed in, the app also starts building the meeting report. After ten seconds of waiting you can choose Keep Waiting or "Tag Now, View Later." If the report lands, you get an editable report review with tags and a share button.
- When you're done, you land in the Review tab on the meeting.

**If something interrupts the mic** (a phone call, another app grabbing audio), a "Recording paused" banner says it resumes on its own. If the app was killed mid-meeting, the next time you open the Copilot tab you'll see a "Meeting interrupted" card with a Resume button, and the part already captured is kept.
