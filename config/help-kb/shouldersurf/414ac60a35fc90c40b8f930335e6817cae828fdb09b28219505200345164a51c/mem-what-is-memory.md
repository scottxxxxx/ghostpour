Memory is the part of Shoulder Surf that remembers across meetings. Its product name is Context Quilt, and the Review tab's Memory view is "Your Context Quilt."

**What it does.** After each meeting (when you're signed in), the transcript goes to Shoulder Surf's servers, where facts are pulled out as "patches": a decision, a commitment with an owner and a deadline, a blocker, a preference someone stated, a person and their role, a goal, a deliverable. Those patches then feed back into answers: live during a meeting, in Meeting Chat and in Project Chat. Ask "what did we promise Dana last month?" and the answer knows, even if nobody said it today.

**What the Memory view shows.** A digest of this week: "N things you're free to forget," a grid of this week's patches, "the thread you're pulling" (the meeting with the most going on), and a panel per project ("What you keep showing up for"). Tap a patch and you're back in the meeting it came from. Tap a project panel for that project's quilt. The "Where does this get used?" pill explains that a chat now knows what you said once.

**Who gets it.** Memory is part of the Plus and Pro plans on Shoulder Surf AI. On Free you get a preview: a purple "N past meetings were relevant" pill on answers when memory would have helped, and an upgrade screen in place of the quilt. People pages are free for every signed-in account.

**What it is not.** It's not a copy of your transcripts you can browse (that's Review), and it's not on your phone (the phone only caches). It's also selective by design: "Nothing worth surfacing this week" means nothing earned a place, not that something broke.
