Shoulder Surf is a meeting copilot that lives on your phone, not on your work computer.

It listens to the meeting through the iPhone's mic (or a few other ways, see "How should I listen?"), writes a live transcript on the device, keeps a rolling summary, and answers questions about what's being said. Answers show up on the phone screen, and you can have them read into your AirPods. The camera can snap slides and screens so the AI can look at them too.

When the meeting ends, the app saves everything to the Review tab: transcript with speaker names, summary, a shareable report, tags, action items, photos, and anything you asked during the call. Over time it builds a memory of the people you meet and the things that get decided, so later answers know the backstory.

**The air gap.** Nothing is installed on your work machine. The app never joins the call and never appears in a screen share. It works for video calls and for people in a room. (The optional Mac and Windows companions are small apps you can install on your own computer to feed call audio straight to the phone; they're there if you want them, and the phone alone works without them.)

**Three tabs.** Copilot (where you record), Review (everything saved), Settings.

**AI you choose.** You can run on Shoulder Surf AI (sign in, free monthly allowance, paid plans for more), on Apple Intelligence on the phone (free, private, smaller), on Apple's cloud model (free, needs iOS 27 and a capable device), or with your own API key from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and others. Settings > AI Model.
