The Free plan on Shoulder Surf AI comes with a monthly allowance of AI usage. When it's used up, here's what changes.

**Starting a meeting.** Tap the orb and instead of recording you get the "AI usage" sheet: "You're out of AI credits," with three ways forward:

- **Subscribe** (Plus or Pro have no monthly cap).
- **Use On-Device AI**, which switches you to Apple Intelligence on the phone. It's greyed out if your device doesn't support Apple Intelligence or it's turned off.
- **Use my own API key**, which takes you to Settings > AI Model.

There's no "record without AI" option on that sheet. Subscribe or Use On-Device AI and recording starts; Use my own API key takes you to Settings first.

**Mid-meeting.** When you're close to the limit you'll see a "Low AI Time" alert with Continue Anyway or Upgrade. If a question is refused because the allowance is gone, the upgrade sheet opens and auto-summaries pause for the rest of the meeting. Your recording and transcript are unaffected; those never use the allowance.

**When Shoulder Surf AI can't be reached** (server trouble, no signal, a timeout): the app retries once, then answers the live question on the phone with Apple Intelligence if your device has it. Those answers are marked "Answered on this iPhone while Shoulder Surf AI is unavailable." The on-device model only sees the newest part of the transcript and no photos, so expect shorter answers. This fallback doesn't kick in when you're signed out or out of allowance.

Your allowance resets monthly. Check it in Settings > Account.
