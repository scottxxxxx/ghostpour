While a meeting is recording, Shoulder Surf shows a Live Activity so you can see it's running without opening the app.

**In the Dynamic Island:** the Shoulder Surf logo, the project name (or "No Project"), a REC or PAUSED badge, and the live timer.

**On the Lock Screen:** the same header plus the last few lines of the rolling summary, or "Listening…" before the first summary lands.

**There are no buttons in it.** You can't pause, stop, mute, or ask a question from the Dynamic Island or Lock Screen, on purpose. A phone in a pocket shouldn't be able to end a recording by brushing against something. Open the app; everything you can do lives there.

If the activity reads "Session ended," the meeting has stopped (or the activity went stale after a long stretch locked). Open the app to check.
