People can show real faces now. Each person's page can pull their card from your Contacts, take a photo you choose, or bring their photo and company over from their LinkedIn profile.

**Photos from Contacts.** People shows an "Add photos from Contacts" card until you answer it. Tap Allow and iOS asks once for Contacts access. The app then matches the people on your list to your contacts and copies each match's photo. "Not now" hides the card; you can still do one person at a time (below).

**How a match is made.** Carefully, because a wrong face is worse than none. A contact you linked by hand always wins. Otherwise it looks for a contact with an email address the person is known by (a linked email, or their address on your calendar invites), then an exact full name, then a first name on its own only when just one contact in your whole address book has it. If two contacts fit equally, nobody gets a photo. It only fills gaps: a photo that's already there is never replaced. Someone new to your list is tried once, the first time you open their page.

**Get or Refresh from Contacts.** On a person's page, tap ⋯ at the top right. "Get from Contacts" (or "Refresh from Contacts" once they're linked) reads their card again and brings in its photo, phone, email and company, keeping anything you typed yourself. If the app can't read Contacts or can't find one sure match, Apple's contact picker opens so you can choose the card.

**Choose Photo.** Same ⋯ menu. Pick any picture from your Photos, then move and scale it inside the circle. Remove Photo takes it off again.

**Get Photo from LinkedIn.** Same ⋯ menu. LinkedIn opens inside the app (sign in to LinkedIn there the first time). Go to the person's profile and tap "Use this profile for <name>." A review card shows what was found, with switches for the photo and the company; if the profile's name doesn't match, it tells you to check. Tap Save and the photo, the company and the profile link are kept. After that the menu offers Open LinkedIn Profile and Remove LinkedIn Link.

**Find on LinkedIn.** Shown when no profile is saved yet. It opens a LinkedIn people search for their name, plus their company when the page knows it, in the LinkedIn app if you have it or in Safari. Nothing comes back from a search; only Get Photo from LinkedIn saves anything.

**Company under their title.** The page shows where they work. Tap it to change it, or tap "Add a company." What you type wins, then the Company field on their linked contact. Failing both, the app guesses from their email's domain or from the address their name carries on the last year of calendar invites (when "Use my calendar" is on), and labels it "Guessed from their email address" or "Guessed from calendar invites." Personal mail like Gmail or iCloud is never used for a guess.

**A to Z sorts like Contacts.** "See all people" follows your iPhone's Contacts sort order: last name first, unless you've set Contacts to sort by first name in iOS Settings. Someone with only one name sorts by that name.

**Privacy.** Contacts are read on your phone. Photos, phone numbers, emails, companies and LinkedIn links stay on your devices and in your private iCloud when iCloud Sync is on, and never go to Shoulder Surf's servers. The LinkedIn page you open in the app talks to LinkedIn like any browser would. You can turn Contacts access off anytime in iOS Settings > Shoulder Surf > Contacts.
