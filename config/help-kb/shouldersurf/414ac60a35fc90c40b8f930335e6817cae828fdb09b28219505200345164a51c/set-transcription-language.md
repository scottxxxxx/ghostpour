The app transcribes on the phone, and the speech engine needs to know which language it's listening for.

**Pick it per meeting.** On the Copilot tab, the globe badge under the orb sets the "Language for this meeting." Do it before you tap the orb. English, Spanish, French, Portuguese and Japanese are offered, limited to what your device's speech engine supports. "What this changes" in the same menu explains the choice.

**The first time you use a language** iOS downloads its speech model, which needs a connection. Recording starts anyway and the app holds on to the audio, so the opening of the meeting is transcribed once the download lands instead of being lost.

**iOS allows five.** An app can keep at most five downloaded speech languages. If you pick a sixth, a "Make room for a language" sheet lists the ones you have, with Remove beside each. Your phone's own language can't be removed. A removed language just downloads again the next time you pick it. Cancel keeps your old choice. If a download doesn't finish in time, a note on the recording screen says which language the meeting is being transcribed in instead.

**Make it stick.** Settings > Audio > Transcription shows the current Meeting Language and a "Remember my choice" switch. Off (the default), every meeting opens in your phone's language and a change lasts one meeting. On, the language you picked stays until you pick another.

**Mixed-language meetings** aren't supported; the engine listens for one language at a time. Pick the one most people will speak.

**Reading in another language afterwards** is separate; see "Reading a meeting in another language."

Two neighbors on the same Settings page: **Filter profanity** (on by default) and **Mark talk-over as Multiple** (on by default; when two people speak at once the label says Multiple instead of guessing).
