The app heard the right words but gave them to the wrong person. A few things cause it, and all of them are fixable after the fact.

**Two people sound alike.** Settings > Voice ID shows "Recognition Warnings" when two enrolled profiles are close. Re-enrolling one of them in a quiet room usually separates them. Name Everywhere in the transcript fixes the meeting you have.

**You aren't labelled "You."** You haven't enrolled, or the owner profile isn't yours. Settings > Voice ID: Enroll Voice, and make sure the profile with the "You" badge is yours ("This Is My Voice" on the right one). On the Mac Companion, you come through the Mac's mic track, which is a more reliable signal than voice matching.

**One voice split into two speakers, or two merged into one.** This happens with speakerphone audio and crosstalk. In the transcript, tap the label and use "This is <name>" or "Name Only This Section" to patch the stretch that's wrong. "Not <name>" unassigns a passage that isn't theirs.

**Talk-over.** When people speak at once the app labels it "Multiple" rather than guessing (Settings > Audio > Mark talk-over as Multiple). Turn it off if you'd rather it pick someone.

**Names went to the wrong person in Memory.** Renaming the speaker in the transcript carries the fix to the meeting's memories. If a first name is ambiguous the app asks "Which <name>?"; pick carefully, and use a last name or nickname for the new person when the name is taken.

**Start over.** ⋯ menu > Restore Original Speaker Labels goes back to Speaker 1, Speaker 2 so you can name from scratch. Re-analyze Meeting afterwards updates the summary with the new names.

The audio itself is always correct; labels are a layer on top, so nothing is lost by fixing them.
