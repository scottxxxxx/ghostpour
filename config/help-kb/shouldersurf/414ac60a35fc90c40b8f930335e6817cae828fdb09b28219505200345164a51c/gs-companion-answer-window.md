With the Mac or Windows Companion, the answer on your phone can also show on your computer, in a see-through window you park over the meeting. You read it like a teleprompter without looking down.

**Turn it on.** On a Mac, open the Companion from the menu bar and switch on "Display responses on this Mac." On Windows, open the tray app and switch on "Display responses on this PC." The window appears while you're recording with your phone connected. If the Mac switch isn't there, connect your phone first: it shows up once a phone that can send answers is linked.

**What it shows.** Whatever answer the phone is showing, including one you pick from the phone's history, filling in as it's written. Back and forward arrows step through this meeting's answers. Drag it anywhere, resize it, and set how see-through it is and how big the text is.

**Cue cards on the Mac.** When an answer comes back as cue cards on the phone, the Mac window shows the same deck, and Control Option Left and Right step through the cards (and past the ends, through the meeting's answers) from any app. That needs Mac Companion 1.0.2 or later. The Windows window shows the full text.

**Kept out of screen shares.** Your audience shouldn't see your notes, so the window stays out of captures:

- On Windows, the system itself leaves it out of screen shares, recordings and screenshots.
- On a Mac, it's marked private. In our tests it didn't appear in a shared screen or a screenshot, but the meeting app has the final say, so if you want to be certain, share a window instead of your whole screen.
- Either way, the Companion never includes it in the screenshots it sends your phone for the camera button.

Questions are still asked on the phone (or with the Mac's Quick Prompt shortcuts); the window only shows answers.
