A meeting recorded in Spanish on an English phone reads in English when you open it. The original is never touched; the translation is a layer you can switch on and off.

**It asks first.** When a meeting was spoken in a different language from your phone's and you're signed in on Shoulder Surf AI, the top of the meeting page asks: "This meeting was in Spanish. Write the summary and report in Spanish or English?" Pick Spanish and the summary and report are written in Spanish, and made if the meeting didn't have them yet. Pick English and the meeting reads in English. Tick "Remember for Spanish meetings" to stop being asked; Settings > Audio > Summary Language lists what you've remembered, each with Ask Again.

**Automatic.** Otherwise, when a meeting's language differs from your phone's and you're signed in, the app builds the translation the first time you open the meeting and shows it. A blue "(translated)" badge appears on the card.

**Switching.** The globe chip beside the tags (and the Language section of the ⋯ menu) lists every language the meeting can be read in. The recording's own language is marked "(matches the audio)." Pick one and the whole meeting flips: transcript, title, summary, report, your questions and the answers. Switching back costs nothing.

**What's translated vs rewritten.** The transcript and your typed questions are translated. The summary, the report and the title are regenerated in the new language from the original transcript, so they read naturally instead of like a translation of a translation. Answers are translated from the language they were given in. Audio and photos stay exactly as recorded; there's no audio translation.

**Sharing in another language.** Both share sheets have a "Send in" picker with every language the app supports, not just the ones you've built, because the recipient's language is a third thing. Export Artifacts translates the text files; Share Meeting translates before the link is made.

**What it needs.** Sign in, and monthly allowance on Shoulder Surf AI: translation runs on the server, so there's no on-device version, and it draws on the same allowance as your questions. Out of allowance, you'll see "translation is paused."

**Other devices.** Which languages a meeting has is synced; the translated text itself is not. Your iPad rebuilds the translation the first time you open it there, which is one quick server call.

**Setting the spoken language.** Pick it on the globe badge under the orb before a meeting. Settings > Audio > Transcription > "Remember my choice" keeps it for the next meeting.
