Once you tap the orb, the Copilot tab turns into the recording screen. Here's the map.

**Top right, left to right:**

- **Listening mode pill.** Shows how the app is listening (iPhone Mic, SS Companion, USB-C, or Read the screen). Tap it to switch mid-meeting.
- **Camera button.** Green when the camera is on, red when off. Tap to turn it off for this meeting (it comes back on next time). The first time you turn it off, a short card explains that photos give the AI something to look at. In Shoulder Surf Companion mode this button instead asks "What am I looking at?" so you can point the AI at your Mac's screen rather than the room.
- **Cable icon.** Only in USB-C mode, to show the cable is live.
- **Mic circle.** Green while audio is flowing, red with a slash when muted. Tap to mute. Muting drops audio from both the recording and the transcript; the timer keeps running.

**Center:** the orb, with the meeting timer under it. Tapping either one opens the pause and end popover. A "Tap the orb to end" hint shows for a few seconds at the start of every recording.

**Bottom toolbar:**

- **Transcript.** Opens the live transcript, with a Summary tab for the rolling summary.
- **Query.** Opens the "Query AI" sheet: Quick Prompts, a custom question, references, how much of the meeting to include, and photos.
- **History.** Every answer from this session, with a count badge.
- **Photos.** Appears once you've captured at least one photo, with a count.

**Anywhere on the camera view:** tap to take a photo. Pinch to zoom. A "Tap anywhere to capture" hint shows until your first capture.

There is no pause or stop button in the toolbar. Those live behind the orb, on purpose, so a stray tap can't end a recording.
