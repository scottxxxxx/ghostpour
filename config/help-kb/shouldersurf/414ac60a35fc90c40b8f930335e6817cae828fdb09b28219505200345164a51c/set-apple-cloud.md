Apple Cloud is Apple's larger model running on Private Cloud Compute, Apple's privacy-preserving servers. It's free to use and sits between the small on-device model and Shoulder Surf AI.

**Requirements.** iOS 27 or later on a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on. It only shows up in Settings > AI Model when the phone qualifies. If it's listed but says "Apple Cloud isn't ready yet," Apple Intelligence is still setting up or is off: iOS Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri.

**Limits.** Apple sets a daily quota. When you hit it, questions fail with "You've reached today's Apple Cloud limit. Switch to another model in Settings, or try again tomorrow," and the app offers Shoulder Surf AI. Rolling summaries spend the same quota every interval. It needs an internet connection. Its context window fits most single meetings but not a whole project.

**What it can do without buying anything:** live questions and Quick Prompts, one photo per question, rolling summaries, Meeting Chat.

**With Extras: Apple Cloud** (one-time purchase in Settings > AI Model): Project Chat on Apple Cloud, attaching files (PDF, Word and RTF are turned into text), post-meeting analysis (sentiment, urgency, tags), streamed answers, more than one photo per question. Owning the pack follows your Apple ID, so it survives signing out.

**What it can't do, ever:** Memory recall in answers, web search, documents read on the server, file generation. Those are Shoulder Surf AI features.

**Privacy.** Questions and attached photos go to Apple's Private Cloud Compute under Apple's terms. Shoulder Surf's servers aren't in that path. Reports, translation and Memory capture still go to Shoulder Surf AI when you're signed in, as with every model.
