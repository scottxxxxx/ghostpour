If you'd rather run on Apple Cloud or your own API key than subscribe, two one-time purchases unlock the features that would otherwise need Shoulder Surf AI. No subscription; buy once, it's yours.

**Extras: Apple Cloud.** Project Chat on Apple Cloud (on Apple's daily quota), attaching files (PDF, Word and RTF turned into text), post-meeting analysis (sentiment, urgency, tags), streamed answers word by word, and more than one photo per question.

**Extras: Own API Key.** Project Chat on your key, original PDFs sent to Claude, Gemini, OpenAI and OpenRouter models, web search through Anthropic's and OpenRouter's search tools (billed to your key), post-meeting analysis, streamed answers, and more than one photo per question.

**Extras: Both** at a lower price than two packs. If you already own one pack, the app offers a cheaper upgrade to the other instead.

**Where to buy.** Settings > AI Model, under the capability rows for Apple Cloud or an own-key provider: "Unlock Extras," one-time purchase, with Restore Purchases beside it. The app also offers the right pack when you bump into a locked feature: opening Project Chat on Apple Cloud, attaching a second photo, attaching a file, turning on web search on your own key.

**What the packs don't include.** Anything that runs on Shoulder Surf's servers: Memory recall in answers, server-side document reading, file generation, reports and translation. Those still need a signed-in Shoulder Surf AI account and use its monthly allowance.

**Ownership.** Tied to your Apple ID's App Store purchases and to your Shoulder Surf account, so a pack survives signing out and reinstalling. Restore Purchases brings it back on a new device. On-device Apple Intelligence has no pack; it's too small for the features a pack would unlock.
