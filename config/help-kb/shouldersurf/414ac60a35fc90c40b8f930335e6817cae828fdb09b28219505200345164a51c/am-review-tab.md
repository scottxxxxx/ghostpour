Everything the app saves lives in Review. A picker at the top switches between four views: **Meetings**, **Projects**, **Memory**, and **People**.

**Meetings.** Grouped by day, newest first. A search field sits above the list and matches as you type across titles, project names, tags, summaries, transcripts, and reports (several words narrow it further). Chips filter by project or tag. Pull down to nudge iCloud sync.

Each card shows the title (and a category line once a report exists), the date and length, the project badge, tag chips, a summary preview, and small counts for words, questions and photos. The emoji at the top right is the meeting's sentiment; the colored orb beside it is urgency. Tap either for a one-line reason. A blue "(translated)" badge means you're reading a translation; a grey "Shared by" badge means it came from someone else. A "Generate full report" button appears on meetings that reached the server but have no report yet.

Swipe a row to delete. Long-press for Rename or Delete. Delete removes the audio, images and transcript from this device (and your iCloud, if sync is on) and can't be undone.

**Import.** The menu at the top of Meetings has "Import a Recording" (audio or video from Files, Voice Memos, the Watch, a meeting platform) and "Import Shared Meeting" (a .shouldersurf file). See "Importing recordings and shared meetings."

**Projects.** Your projects as cards, with a + to create one. Open a project for its meetings, notes, action items, people and Project Chat.

**Memory.** Your Context Quilt: what the app has learned across meetings this week, and per project.

**People.** Everyone your meetings have mentioned, with a page each.

Tap a meeting to open its page. See "The meeting page."
