Meetings end and phones stay in pockets. The silence watchdog is the app's way of asking "are you still there?" so a forgotten recording doesn't run all afternoon, heat the phone, and burn AI time on empty summaries.

**How it works.** The app watches for new speech in the transcript. When nothing new has arrived for the time you chose, it fires once. It fires again only after someone speaks and then goes quiet again, so it won't nag.

**The setting.** Settings > Audio > Silence Watchdog > **Alert After**: 2, 5, 10, 15 or 20 minutes, or Never. The default is 5 minutes.

**When the phone is locked or you're in another app,** you get a notification: "No Speech Detected. No one has spoken for N minutes. Would you like to stop the session?" with two buttons, **Keep Recording** and **Stop Session**. Stop Session ends the meeting right from the notification, saves it, and the post-meeting analysis still runs with tags applied automatically. You'll find the meeting in Review.

**While the app is open on the recording screen,** the watchdog still notices silence and pauses the auto-summaries, but it doesn't show an alert on screen in the current layout. If you want the nudge, lock the phone or switch apps.

Pause turns the watchdog off (you meant to stop capturing), and it resets when you resume.

The watchdog is the only thing in the app that reacts to silence, and the only thing it can do is ask. It never stops a recording on its own.
