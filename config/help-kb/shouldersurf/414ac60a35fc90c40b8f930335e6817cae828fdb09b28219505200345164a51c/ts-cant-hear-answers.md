title: Play is greyed out, or I can't hear answers
category: Troubleshooting
keywords: [can't hear, play greyed out, play disabled, no sound, airpods not connected, speaker playback, route, voiceover, mute, wired headphones]
screen: session
---

**Play is greyed out.** Nothing is wrong with the answer; the app has nowhere to send the audio. By default it only plays through Bluetooth headphones. Either connect AirPods (or any Bluetooth headset), or turn on Settings > Audio > Output > Speaker Playback to allow the phone speaker and wired headphones. The warning under that switch is real: with the mic unmuted, the speaker's words can end up in your transcript.

**AirPods are connected to my Mac, not the phone.** The app won't pull them over. Tap the route button beside Play (or open Control Center) and pick the iPhone.

**AirPods just connected and still nothing.** The app waits a couple of seconds for a fresh connection. Press Play again.

**It's playing, but into the room.** Speaker Playback is on and no headphones are connected. Put headphones in, or turn the switch off.

**USB-C cable mode.** Answers never go down the cable. They play through the speaker if Speaker Playback is on, otherwise not at all.

**VoiceOver is on.** The app doesn't read answers aloud while VoiceOver is running, so the two don't talk over each other. VoiceOver reads the answer text itself.

**Nothing happens automatically.** That's by design. Answers never auto-play; press Play on the answer, or the Play button on the Watch.

**I muted Bluetooth audio ages ago.** If Play stays disabled with AirPods connected and Speaker Playback on, an old "Mute Bluetooth audio" setting from a previous layout may be stuck on. Turning Speaker Playback off and on, then reconnecting the headphones, is worth a try; if it persists, contact support.
